What works here?

  • Frequency and percentage tables
  • Weighted mean and standard deviation
  • Median, mode and top response
  • Calculation formulas and CSV export

Important limitations

Summary frequencies cannot support correlations, regressions, t-tests, ANOVA, or Cronbach's alpha, because answers are not paired across items.

The full Python Data View, Variable View, and advanced statistical engine are not hosted on this Worker. This is an explicitly limited, usable Cloudflare preview.
