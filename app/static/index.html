STUDY DESIGN · BEFORE DATA COLLECTION

Power & Sample Size Planner

Plan how many completed observations your proposed statistical design needs. Calculations use Statsmodels and your explicitly assumed effect, not observed results.

Local Python edition
A priori planning only. A sample-size calculation cannot establish that your study will discover an effect. You must justify your chosen effect size, alpha, power target, study design and expected attrition before collecting results.

Define your proposed study

No questionnaire file, respondent-level dataset or frequency counts are required or accepted.

DESIGN

What these numbers mean

Power is a planning probability under your assumed population effect and test. It does not predict a particular study's p-value or substitute for sampling representativeness.

Choose your effect before collecting data

Prefer prior evidence, a justified minimum scientifically important effect, or a pilot estimate with uncertainty. Do not use a fabricated value merely to obtain a smaller sample.

Methods and limits

Independent t planning assumes pooled equal variance; the analysis workbench's Welch test is different. Proportion planning uses a large-sample normal approximation. ANOVA assumes balanced groups.
